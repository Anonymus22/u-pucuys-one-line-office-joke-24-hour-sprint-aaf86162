# One-Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint

A single original one-line joke about office life, written for the make.fun "One-Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint" bounty. The joke finds its humour in the most neglected corner of the modern office — the communal fridge — where a yoghurt abandoned since 2023 has somehow outlasted every round of reorganisations, acquiring its own email thread, a meeting invite, and more job security than the person who left it there. The program writes exactly that one line to `/output/entry.txt`.
